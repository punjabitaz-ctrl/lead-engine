# 72-hour delivery checklist

**Hour 0:** payment received, intake complete, agreement signed. `deal move <campaign> <deal> Won`. Start the clock.
**Hour 0-24:** page structure and copy from the intake; light edits only. Build on your template.
**Hour 24-48:** design pass, mobile first. Add map, hours, click-to-call, contact form (name, phone, general reason only; no health details). Basic privacy notice for 3-page.
**Hour 48:** send the preview link. Client has until hour 60 to send one round of changes.
**Hour 60-72:** apply changes; QA; launch; handoff.

**QA before launch**
* Mobile and desktop; Chrome and Safari
* Mobile speed test passes (target 80+); images compressed
* Phone number, address, hours and doctor names match the intake exactly
* Contact form delivers to the right inbox; no health fields; spam protection on
* Title, description, and local business markup set; Google Business Profile linked
* No placeholder text; no broken links; privacy notice present
* Domain, SSL (https) and email forwarding work

**Handoff:** send logins or files, a one-page "how to update", and the monthly plan offer. Ask for a Google review and a referral. `deal move <campaign> <deal> Delivered`.
