# Intake form (15 minutes)

Start of the 72 hours. Send as a short form; do not ask for anything about patients.

1. Practice name as it should appear, and the doctors' or clinicians' names and credentials
2. Address, phone, email for the site, hours (and holiday hours)
3. Services to list (up to 8), and anything you do NOT want listed
4. Insurance accepted (optional) and languages spoken
5. Logo and brand colors (upload), 3-6 photos of the office or team if you have them
6. Your Google Business Profile link (or: "I do not have one")
7. Your domain name and where it is registered (or: "I need one")
8. Two websites you like, and one you dislike
9. Anything we should say about how new patients can book (call, form, online scheduler link)
10. Who approves the final site, and by when
