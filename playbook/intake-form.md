# Intake form (about 15 minutes)

Work does not start, and the 72-hour clock does not run, until every item below is supplied. Do not ask for anything about patients.

**Required (the 2-3 hour build depends on these)**
1. **Logo and branding:** logo files, brand colors, fonts if any
2. **Imagery:** 3-6 photos of the office or team, and any other images to use
3. **Text copy:** practice description, services (up to 8), doctors' or clinicians' names and credentials, "about" text
4. **Videos** (if any): links or files
5. **Legal or other business data:** legal practice name, address, phone, email for the site, hours (and holiday hours), license or registration text you want shown, insurance accepted
6. **Privacy and data-retention policy:** supplied by you. If you do not have one, a drafted version from my template can be quoted separately for your attorney to review; work starts once it exists.

**Also helpful**
7. Your Google Business Profile link (or "I do not have one")
8. Your domain name and where it is registered (or "I need one")
9. Two websites you like, and one you dislike
10. How new patients should book (call, form, scheduler link)
11. Who approves the final site, and by when

When all items are in: `leadengine campaign deal intake <campaign> <deal>`.
