# Follow-up emails

You approve each send. Keep them under 100 words. Keep the opt-out line and your postal address on every first-contact email.

**After a call (same day)**
> Subject: Your website, as discussed
> Hi [Name], thanks for the call. As promised: [one-page proposal attached / mock-up link]. One page is $250 (paid up front), three pages $500 (50% deposit), live 72 hours after payment and your materials.
> If it suits, reply "yes" and I'll send the agreement and the short intake form.  [Your name]

**Silence after the proposal (day 3)**
> Subject: Re: Your website
> Hi [Name], a quick check on the proposal. If the timing is wrong, tell me and I'll check back in [month].  [Your name]

**Last note (day 10)**
> Subject: Closing the loop
> Hi [Name], I'll stop here so I don't clutter your inbox. If a new site becomes a priority, I'm at [email / phone].  [Your name]

**Won (after payment)**
> Subject: Welcome: next step is a 15-minute form
> Hi [Name], payment received, thank you. Your 72 hours starts when I have everything on this list: [intake form]. I'll send a preview at hour 48.  [Your name]
