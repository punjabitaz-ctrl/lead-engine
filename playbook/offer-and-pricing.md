# Offer and pricing

## Products (set in the campaign's `spec.json`, `offer.products`)

| ID | Product | Price | Included |
|---|---|---|---|
| `page1` | Single-page site | $250 | one mobile-friendly page: practice name, services, location and map, hours, phone, contact form (no patient details), Google Business Profile link |
| `page3` | 3-page basic site | $500 | home, services, about/contact, same extras, a basic privacy notice |

**The 72-hour clock** starts when payment is received and the intake form is complete, not when the call ends.

**Not included** (offered separately, priced when asked): logo or brand design, copywriting beyond light edits, photography, online booking, patient portals,
e-commerce, forms that collect health information, more than one round of revisions, ongoing edits.

## Recurring offer (decide this; it decides the economics)

At $250-$500 a one-off site does not pay for the time spent winning it (see `docs/EXECUTIVE_REVIEW.md`). A monthly plan is what makes the work worthwhile.
Define one before the first call:

* Name: [Care plan]  Price: [$__/month]  Includes: [hosting and security updates, up to __ edits a month, Google Business Profile upkeep, review requests, monthly traffic note]
* Start with a single plan and test one price. Ask your first five prospects what they pay today for upkeep and who does it, then set the price from what they say.

## Rules

1. **Capacity: 10 projects a week at most** (`offer.capacity_per_week`). When 10 have started in the ISO week, outreach pauses (`campaign status` says so).
2. **Remote first.** Email, phone, video. Never a standard in-person step.
3. **In-person only when a deal is worth $5,000 a month or more** (`offer.in_person_min_monthly_value`). The pipeline report lists the deals that qualify. Under that line, do it remotely.
4. **Payment:** $250 in full before work; $500 as 50% deposit and 50% on launch [your call]. Card or invoice link.
5. **No patient information** is collected through the sites you build, and you do not sign BAAs. Say so in the proposal.
6. **Proof:** show 2-3 examples. A medical-practice example (even a self-made demo site) beats a general portfolio.
