# Website privacy and data-retention policy (template for attorney review)

> A draft for the client's attorney to review and adjust. It is not legal advice and not a HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices. Fill the brackets; delete what does not
> apply. Describe only what the site actually does: if the site has no analytics, remove that section.

**[Practice legal name]** ("we") operates [website address]. Effective date: [date]. Contact: [email], [phone], [postal address].

## 1. Please do not send health information through this website
The contact form and email links on this site are not secure channels for medical information. Do not include symptoms, diagnoses, medications, insurance details or other
health information. To discuss care, call us at [phone]. In an emergency, call 911.

## 2. What we collect
* **Information you give us:** if you use the contact form, your name, phone number, email address and the general reason for contacting us.
* **Automatically collected:** [if applicable] standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited, date and time) and, if analytics is enabled, cookie-based usage statistics.
* **Cookies:** [list: strictly necessary only | analytics | none]. [How to turn them off].

## 3. How we use it
To reply to your message, to schedule or confirm contact, to keep the site secure, and to understand how the site is used so we can improve it. We do not sell personal information.

## 4. Who receives it
Service providers that host the site, deliver email, or provide analytics ([names]), acting for us; and authorities where the law requires. We do not share contact-form details for advertising.

## 5. How long we keep it (data retention)
| Data | Kept for | Then |
|---|---|---|
| Contact-form messages and the emails they create | [12] months, or until the matter is resolved | deleted |
| Server and security logs | [30-90] days | deleted automatically |
| Analytics data | [14] months [or the shortest setting your tool allows] | deleted automatically |
| Records we must keep by law | the period the law requires | deleted when it ends |
You can ask us to delete your message sooner (section 7).

## 6. Security
We use reasonable safeguards (encrypted connections, access limited to people who need it, software kept up to date). No method of sending information over the internet is completely secure.

## 7. Your choices
You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold from this website, or to stop contacting you, by emailing [email]. We will respond within [30] days.

### Notes for the reviewing attorney (delete before publishing)
* A HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices is a separate document the practice may owe patients; this web policy does not replace it.
* Check whether any state privacy law (for example New Jersey's data privacy statute) applies to this practice, given its thresholds and any health-data exemptions.
* Confirm retention periods against the practice's record-keeping duties and its insurer's guidance.
* If analytics, a scheduler, chat or email-marketing tools are added, this policy and the HIPAA analysis change. Those are out of scope of the $250/$500 builds.
* Accessibility and cookie-consent rules are separate from this policy.
