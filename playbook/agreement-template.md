# Website Services Agreement (starting draft)

> Starting point only. Have a New Jersey attorney review and adjust it before use. Fill the brackets.

This agreement is between **[Your business name]** ("Designer", [address]) and **[Practice legal name]** ("Client", [address]), dated [date].

1. **Scope.** Designer will build the website described in the attached proposal ("Work"): [single-page | 3-page] site. Anything not listed is out of scope and quoted separately.
2. **Price and payment.** $[250 | 500]. [Single-page site: paid in full before any work begins | 3-page site: a 50% deposit ($250) before any work begins and the balance on launch]. **No work begins until payment is received.** Late amounts: [interest or pause of services].
3. **Timeline and client materials.** Designer will deliver a working site within 72 hours after (a) the payment required before work begins and (b) Client's delivery of **all** of the following: logo and branding, imagery, text copy, videos (if any), legal or other business data, and Client's privacy and data-retention policy. Missing or late materials pause the clock; Designer does not write legal policies.
4. **Revisions.** One round of revisions is included, requested within [5] days of delivery. Further changes are billed at [$__/hour] or covered by a monthly plan.
5. **Client materials.** Client confirms it owns or may use all text, logos and images it supplies, and that the content is accurate.
6. **No patient information.** The site's forms are not designed for health information. Client will not use them to collect or transmit protected health information. Designer does not provide HIPAA compliance services and will not sign a business associate agreement. If Client needs those, the parties will agree a separate scope.
7. **Ownership.** On full payment, Client owns the Work and its content. Designer keeps ownership of its pre-existing tools and may show the site in a portfolio unless Client objects in writing.
8. **Domain and hosting.** Client owns its domain. [Designer hosts the site for $__/month | Client arranges hosting]. If Designer hosts, Client may move the site at any time and Designer will provide the files.
9. **Care plan (optional).** If chosen: [Basic upkeep $9.99/month: keeps the site online and secure, no changes | Upkeep + 1 change $20/month: upkeep and one change or update a month of up to 30 minutes of work | Priority upkeep $50/month: tier 1 support, advanced upkeep, up to 5 changes a month, each up to 190 minutes, and no more than 4 hours of work in total a month]. Unused changes and time do not roll over. Month-to-month; either party may cancel on 30 days' notice.
9A. **Work beyond the plan.** Any change that exceeds a plan's limits is separate paid work under a written quote of [$__/hour or a fixed price] that Client approves before work begins. Ownership of the deliverables of that work passes to Client on payment in full. [Attorney: confirm the ownership wording; "work made for hire" has a specific legal meaning and may not fit.]
9B. **Privacy policy.** Client supplies its own privacy and data-retention policy, or buys the drafting add-on; either way Client is responsible for having its attorney review it. Designer does not give legal advice.
10. **Warranty.** Designer will fix defects reported within [30] days of launch. Otherwise the Work is provided as is; Designer does not guarantee search ranking, traffic or patient volume.
11. **Limitation of liability.** Designer's total liability is limited to the amount Client paid under this agreement. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential damages.
12. **Cancellation and refunds.** Before work starts: full refund. After work starts: [refund of the unused portion / no refund of the deposit].
13. **Governing law.** New Jersey. Disputes: [courts of ___ County, NJ].
14. **Entire agreement.** This agreement and the proposal are the whole agreement; changes must be in writing.

Signed: ______________ (Designer)   ______________ (Client)   Date: ________
