# Call script (only when a call is required: Fridays 10:00-15:00; no cold calls)

**Goal:** find out whether they need a site or a better one, and either book the proposal or close the loop politely. Not to demo or to negotiate on the first call.

1. **Open (10 s):** "Hi, is this [Dr./Name]? I'm [you], a web designer based in [town]. I'm calling about the website for [practice]. Do you have 60 seconds?"
   * Gatekeeper: "I'm following up on an email about [practice]'s website; who looks after that?" Get a name and a best time. Do not pitch to the front desk.
2. **Reason for calling (15 s):** use the lead's signal.
   * No site/profile: "I searched for [practice] in [town] and couldn't find a site or a claimed Google listing."
   * Weak site: "I looked at [website] on a phone and [issue]."
   * Thin reviews: "You have [n] Google reviews; most practices nearby have more."
3. **Questions (2 min):** How do most new patients find you today? Is the site something you update yourself? Anything you have wanted to fix? Who decides on this? What did you try before?
4. **Offer (1 min):** "I build a clean, mobile-friendly site in 72 hours once I have payment and your details: one page for $250 paid up front, or three pages for $500 with a 50% deposit. Upkeep plans start at $9.99 a month. No patient information is collected through the site."
5. **Next step (30 s):** "I can send a one-page proposal and a quick homepage mock-up today. What email should I use?" Book a 10-minute follow-up.
6. **Close the loop:** if not interested, ask: "What would have to be true for this to matter?" Log the answer as the Lost reason.

After the call: `leadengine campaign deal move <campaign> <deal> "Call booked"` (or Replied / Lost with a reason).

Before calling: check the National Do-Not-Call Registry and your state's rules; business lines are treated differently from cell phones. Call during business hours only.
