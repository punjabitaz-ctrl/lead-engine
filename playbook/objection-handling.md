# Objections

| They say | Respond | Log as |
|---|---|---|
| "We already have a website." | "Great. How does it look on a phone, and how many new patients does it bring in? I'm happy to send a two-line note on what I'd change; no charge." | Has site |
| "Send me information." | "Of course. To make it useful: do you want pricing, examples, or a mock-up of your homepage?" Send the one-page proposal, not a brochure. | Info requested |
| "How much?" | "$250 for a one-page site, $500 for three pages, live in 72 hours once I have your details. Hosting and upkeep is [$__/month], optional." | Price asked |
| "Too expensive." | Ask what they compare it to. Offer the one-page site. Do not discount; remove scope instead. | Price |
| "We're too busy." | "That's why it is 72 hours and you only need to spend 15 minutes on the intake form." | Timing |
| "My nephew / agency does it." | "Good. If it's ever not getting done, I'm a message away." Ask permission to check back in 3 months. | Has provider |
| "Is it HIPAA compliant?" | "The sites don't collect patient information; the contact form asks for name, phone and a general reason only. I don't handle protected health information or sign BAAs." | Compliance |
| "Who owns it?" | "You do. The site and domain are yours when paid; I can host it or hand it over." | Ownership |
| "Not now." | "Understood. May I follow up in [month]?" Set a reminder. | Not now |
| "Stop contacting me." | "Understood, I'll remove you." Add to `suppress.csv` immediately. | Opted out |
