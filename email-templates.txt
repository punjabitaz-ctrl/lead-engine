CAN-SPAM COMPLIANCE NOTES
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Every email must include:
✓ Valid physical address (footer)
✓ Clear unsubscribe mechanism (or "stop replying")
✓ Identification as an advertisement/promotional message
✓ Accurate subject line (no deception)

Footer template:
"AYiN Advisors | [Physical Address] | www.ayinadvisors.com
You received this email because you matched our criteria. Reply STOP to unsubscribe."
