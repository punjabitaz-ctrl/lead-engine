# Find Qualified Leads for Medical Practices
## Fast, verified, human-approved outreach that fills your pipeline.

---

### The Problem

Medical practices struggle to find qualified leads because:
- Public registries (NPPES) don't have emails or websites
- Google Places data is incomplete and unverified
- Manual research takes hours per practice
- Cold emails bounce or go to spam without proper setup

**Result:** Practices sit empty while competitors capture their patients.

---

### The Solution

Lead Engine finds, verifies, scores, and delivers qualified leads with zero cold calling. We use public registries (NPPES), Google Places API, and job postings — then you approve every email before it sends.

**Result:** A pipeline of verified prospects ready for your outreach.

---

### How It Works

1. **Discover** — Pull from NPPES, Google Places, job boards (no scraping)
2. **Score & Filter** — Drop non-buyers using size, signals, and presence checks
3. **Human Gate** — You review batches: Keep / Drop / Unsure
4. **Paced Outreach** — Warm-up emails with bounce handling and suppression lists
5. **Track Everything** — Reports by source, signal, and revenue
