The engine in action.

Discover

We pull from public registries (NPPES), Google Places, job postings and company lists — no scraping, no guessing. Every lead is verified at source.

Score & Filter

We apply signals (hiring posts, missing websites, thin reviews) and size thresholds to drop non-buyers. Only qualified prospects move forward.

Human Gate

You review each batch before anyone is contacted. Keep / Drop / Unsure decisions ensure every email sent is intentional and approved.

Paced Outreach

Dated tasks with warm-up pacing, bounce handling, and suppression lists built in. Track replies, bookings and revenue by source.
